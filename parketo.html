Parketo

Park it perfectly · Developer: Hovsep Hovsepyan (Corvexo Games)

Parketo is a game of short parking puzzles with realistic car physics. Squeeze into tight spots and score points for precision.

  • Reverse, parallel and tight-spot parking challenges
  • Realistic car physics
  • Score points for precision
  • Fill your garage with new cars, from compact hatchbacks to long pickups and buses

Optional in-app purchases and video ads. The game can be completed without paying.

Support

Questions, bug reports or purchase problems? Email corvexogames@gmail.com. Purchases such as Remove Ads are restored automatically when you reinstall the game with the same Apple ID or Google account. If a purchase is missing, write to us.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

  1. What we collect. The game itself does not require an account and does not collect your name, email or location. Game progress is stored on your device.
  2. Advertising. The game shows rewarded and interstitial video ads through Unity LevelPlay and its partner networks. These partners may collect your device advertising identifier (on iOS the IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, device model and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. On iOS you can refuse tracking in the prompt or in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  3. Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.
  4. Analytics and diagnostics. We may receive anonymous diagnostic data (crashes, performance) to improve the game.
  5. Children. The game is not directed to children under 13.
  6. Your choices. Removing the app deletes locally stored progress. You can also request deletion of your data. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
  7. Contact. corvexogames@gmail.com
