Privacy Policy

Corvexo Games · Effective date: September 22, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play games published by Corvexo Games ("we", "us") on Google Play and the App Store, and how that information is used. It applies to all of our games.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, or other directly identifying information inside our games. Our games use third-party services that may automatically collect:

  • Device information such as model, operating system version, language, and screen size.
  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / IDFA) and app installation IDs.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped.
  • Gameplay and usage data, such as levels played, session length, and in-game events.
  • Crash and performance data.

Third-party services

These services process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) ad mediation and its ad network partners — privacy policy
  • Unity Analytics and Unity Diagnostics (Unity Technologies) — privacy policy
  • Google Play Billing and Google Play Services — privacy policy

How the information is used

  • To show ads, which keep our games free.
  • To understand how the game is played and improve its design and balance.
  • To fix bugs and improve game performance.
  • To process and restore in-app purchases.

We do not sell your personal information.

Children

Some of our games may be played by children under 13. When a user may be a child, personalized advertising is disabled and ads are served in a way that complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy. Analytics data from these users is used only to operate and improve the game, not for advertising. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

In-app purchases

Our games offer optional in-app purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store; we never receive your payment card or billing details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so the item can be delivered in the game. Parents can control purchases with Google Play parental controls or purchase authentication.

Your choices

  • You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your device settings to opt out of personalized ads.
  • You can request deletion of any data associated with your device by emailing us.

Data retention and security

Data collected by third-party services is retained according to their policies. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
