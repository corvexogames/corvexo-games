Privacy Policy

Developer: Hovsep Hovsepyan (Corvexo Games) · Effective date: November 5, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the following mobile games published on Google Play and the Apple App Store by the developer Hovsep Hovsepyan, an independent developer working under the studio name Corvexo Games ("we", "us"):

  • Nebula Blast
  • Little Orbit
  • Orbital Siege

It explains what information is collected when you play these games and how that information is used.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, or other directly identifying information inside our games. Your game progress and settings are saved locally on your device. Our games use third-party services that may automatically collect:

  • Device information such as model, operating system version, language, and screen size.
  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID (Android Advertising ID, or on iOS the IDFA, only if you allow tracking), the identifier for vendors (IDFV) on iOS, and app installation IDs.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped.
  • Gameplay and usage data, such as levels played, session length, and in-game events.
  • Crash and performance data.
  • If you sign in to Google Play Games: your Google Play Games player ID, player name and avatar, and the scores and achievements you submit to leaderboards.
  • If you make a purchase: a purchase confirmation (order ID, product and time). We never receive your payment details.

Third-party services

We share the data listed above only with the following service providers, which process it on our behalf and under their own privacy policies. We work only with providers that protect this data to the same or an equal standard as this policy:

  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) ad mediation and its ad network partners (Unity Technologies) — privacy policy
  • Unity Analytics and Unity Diagnostics (Unity Technologies) — privacy policy
  • Google Play Games Services (leaderboards and achievements), Google Play Billing and Google Play Services (Google LLC) — privacy policy
  • App Store and in-app purchases on iOS (Apple Inc.) — privacy policy

How the information is used

  • To show ads, which keep our games free.
  • To understand how the game is played and improve its design and balance.
  • To fix bugs and improve game performance.
  • To process and restore in-app purchases.
  • To show leaderboards and achievements when you use Google Play Games.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone other than the service providers listed above, except when required by law.

Tracking on iOS

On iPhone and iPad, we use Apple's App Tracking Transparency. Before any ads are loaded, the game asks for your permission to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you allow it, the IDFA is shared with Unity LevelPlay and its ad network partners to show personalized ads and measure them. If you decline, the IDFA is not available to us or to them, and you will see non-personalized ads instead. The game works the same either way. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

Some of our games may be played by children under 13. When a user may be a child, personalized advertising is disabled and ads are served in a way that complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Google Play's Families Policy and the Apple App Store Review Guidelines. Analytics data from these users is used only to operate and improve the game, not for advertising. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

In-app purchases

Our games offer optional in-app purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store; we never receive your payment card or billing details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so the item can be delivered in the game. Parents can control purchases with Google Play parental controls or purchase authentication, and on iOS with Screen Time and Ask to Buy.

Your choices

  • On Android, you can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings to opt out of personalized ads.
  • On iOS, you can allow or withdraw tracking permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can sign out of Google Play Games at any time; your scores will then no longer be submitted.
  • You can request access to or deletion of the data associated with you. See Delete your data for how to do this. We complete requests within 30 days.
  • If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region with similar laws, you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Data retention and security

Data collected by third-party services is retained according to their policies, or until you ask us to delete it. Data is sent to these services over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Uninstalling a game deletes the progress saved on your device. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

This website

Our website counts page views and clicks on its buttons and links (for example, which game's Google Play button was pressed) to see which games interest visitors. This is done on our own server, without cookies and without third-party analytics. We store the page, the button, the referring website and the device type (phone, tablet or computer). We do not store your IP address; a visitor is counted with a one-day anonymous code that cannot be linked to you or across days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
