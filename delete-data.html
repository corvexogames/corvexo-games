Delete your data

Corvexo Games · Applies to all Corvexo Games apps on Google Play

Our games do not have their own accounts. You can ask us to delete the data associated with you at any time, and we'll handle the request within 30 days.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to corvexogames@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Include the name of the game and, if you use it, your Google Play Games player name, so we can find your records.
  3. We'll confirm by email once your data has been deleted.

What is deleted

  • Gameplay analytics collected through Unity Analytics for your device.
  • Your scores on our Google Play Games leaderboards.
  • Any emails you have sent us, after the request is completed.

What is kept

  • Purchase records are held by Google Play, not by us, and are kept according to Google's policies and legal requirements.
  • Ad data is processed by Unity LevelPlay and its ad partners. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads) to stop it from being linked to you.
  • Your Google Play Games profile is managed by Google. You can delete it in the Google Play Games app settings.

Retention

Deletion requests are completed within 30 days. Aggregated statistics that cannot identify you may be retained. Anonymous crash and diagnostic data is automatically deleted by Unity according to its retention period.
