Orbital Siege: Tower Defense

Defend the orbital base · Developer: Hovsep Hovsepyan (Corvexo Games)

Orbital Siege is a sci-fi tower defense game. The invasion fleet is coming for your station, and you decide where to build.

  • 20 handcrafted maps in two sectors, each with its own route layout
  • 7 towers: Railgun, Pulse Laser, Flak, Cryo, EMP, Plasma Mortar and Support Node
  • Ground and air enemies, shields, armor and sector bosses
  • Upgrade towers and research permanent bonuses with campaign stars
  • Three game speeds (x1, x2, x3)
  • Earn up to 3 stars on every map

Optional in-app purchases and rewarded video ads. The game can be completed without paying.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

  1. What we collect. The game itself does not require an account and does not collect your name, email or location. Game progress is stored on your device.
  2. Advertising. The game shows rewarded and interstitial video ads through Unity LevelPlay and its partner networks (AppLovin, Mintegral, Unity Ads). These partners may collect your device advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt), IP address, device model and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. You can refuse tracking in the iOS prompt or in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  3. Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.
  4. Analytics and diagnostics. We may receive anonymous diagnostic data (crashes, performance) to improve the game.
  5. Children. The game is not directed to children under 13.
  6. Your choices. You can disable tracking at any time in iOS settings. Removing the app deletes locally stored progress. You can also request deletion of your data.
  7. Contact. corvexogames@gmail.com
