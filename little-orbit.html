Little Orbit

Defend your tiny core · Developer: Hovsep Hovsepyan (Corvexo Games)

Little Orbit is an arcade roguelite. Waves of space invaders are closing in on your tiny core, and every run you build a new defence around it.

  • Pick upgrade cards each run and build up your orbiting defences
  • Waves of invaders and boss fights
  • Power up between runs in the Workshop
  • Daily supplies and rewards

Optional in-app purchases and video ads. The game can be completed without paying.
